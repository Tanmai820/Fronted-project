Welcome to Our Green Campus

Building a clean, green and sustainable future.

🏫 College Introduction

Our college is committed to creating a clean, healthy and environmentally friendly campus. We encourage students and staff to participate in activities that protect nature.

Our Green Campus initiative focuses on renewable energy, tree plantation, waste management, water conservation and maintaining a clean environment.

🌿 Green Campus Activities

🌳 Tree Plantation

Students and staff plant trees around the campus to increase greenery and improve air quality.

💧 Rainwater Harvesting

Rainwater is collected and stored for future use and to conserve water.

🧹 Clean Campus Drive

Regular cleaning activities are conducted to keep the campus clean and healthy.

♻️ Recycling

Waste materials are separated and recyclable materials are sent for recycling.

☀️ Solar Energy

Clean Energy for Our Campus

Solar panels are used to generate electricity from sunlight. Solar energy is renewable and helps reduce dependence on conventional energy.

  • Reduces electricity consumption
  • Uses renewable energy
  • Reduces pollution
  • Helps protect the environment

♻️ Waste Management

🥬 Wet Waste

Food waste and other biodegradable waste can be converted into compost.

📦 Dry Waste

Paper, plastic, glass and other recyclable materials are separated for recycling.

💻 E-Waste

Electronic waste is collected separately and disposed of through proper recycling methods.
