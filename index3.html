🏫 College Introduction

Our college is committed to creating a clean, healthy and environmentally friendly campus. We encourage students and staff to participate in activities that protect nature and promote sustainability.

A Greener Campus

Our Green Campus initiative focuses on renewable energy, tree plantation, water conservation, recycling and maintaining a clean environment.


By working together, students, teachers and staff can create a healthier environment for everyone.

🌿 Green Campus Activities

We conduct various activities to make our campus environmentally friendly.

🌳

Tree Plantation

Planting trees helps improve air quality and increases greenery around the campus.

💧

Rainwater Harvesting

Rainwater is collected and stored to conserve water and reduce wastage.

🧹

Clean Campus Drive

Students participate in cleaning activities to keep the campus clean and healthy.

♻️

Recycling

Recyclable materials are separated and sent for proper recycling.

☀️ Solar Energy

Clean and Renewable Energy

Solar panels convert sunlight into electricity. Solar energy is a clean and renewable source of energy that can help reduce pollution.

  • Reduces electricity consumption
  • Uses renewable energy
  • Reduces pollution
  • Protects the environment
  • Promotes sustainable development

♻️ Waste Management

Proper waste management helps maintain a clean campus and protects the environment.

🥬

Wet Waste

Food waste and biodegradable materials can be converted into compost.

📦

Dry Waste

Paper, plastic, glass and other recyclable materials are separated for recycling.

💻

E-Waste

Electronic waste is collected separately and sent for proper disposal.
